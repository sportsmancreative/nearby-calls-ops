# Onboarding playbook

This is the order we onboard a new Nearby Calls client. It's a first draft built from our first client (Irwin Benefits Consulting, Nov 2026). Steps marked **(to confirm)** still need Jordan to say how we do it today. Update this file whenever we learn a better way.

## 0. Before you start
- Client has paid (checkout.nearbycalls.com) or Jordan has said "start".
- Create their file: copy `clients/_template.md` to `clients/<business-name>.md`.

## 1. Welcome and intake
- Send the welcome message (Claude drafts it, a person approves and sends).
- Collect the intake info in **Intake checklist** below. Ask for everything in one message so the client isn't chased five times.
- Ask the client to add us as a **Manager** on their Google Business Profile **(to confirm: which Google account we use)**.

### Intake checklist (ask the client)
- Business legal name (exactly as on IRS paperwork) and any "doing business as" name
- Business type (LLC, corporation, sole proprietor, partnership, nonprofit)
- EIN: ask them to **type it into GHL themselves or give it to us by phone**, never by email or text. If they have the IRS letter (CP-575 or 147C), a photo of it helps, sent the same safe way.
- Business address (no P.O. box), website, main phone
- Main contact: first and last name, job title, email, mobile number
- Who should get review requests (customers after a job, past customers, both) and how they'll tell us (we add them, they add them, an app sends them)
- Logo, a few photos, hours, services, service area

## 2. GHL sub-account
- Create the client's sub-account in the Nearby Calls agency GHL **(to confirm: from which snapshot)**.
- Fill in business info: name, address, phone, website, time zone, logo.
- Add the client as a user if they'll use the app **(to confirm)**.
- Get a texting number: **Settings > Phone System > Phone Numbers > Add Number**. Pick a local number (417 or their area code).

## 3. Texting registration (A2P 10DLC)
Required before any texts go out, including review requests by text. See `playbook/a2p-10dlc.md`. It can take a week or more, so **start it on day one**.

## 4. Google Business Profile
- Accept the Manager invite. Connect the profile in GHL: **Settings > Integrations > Google** (also used for reviews).
- Set up Paige (Merchynt) for the client **(to confirm steps)**.
- Note the profile's current state in the client file (rating, number of reviews, categories) so we can show progress later.

## 5. Review requests
See `playbook/reviews.md`. Email requests can start as soon as Google is connected. Text requests wait for A2P approval.

## 6. Go-live check
- Send yourself (a team member) a test review request by email and by text. Click the link and check it opens the client's Google review form.
- Tell the client what's running and what they need to do (usually: tell us or the system who their customers are).
- Mark the client "Live" in their file.

## 7. After onboarding (to confirm)
Monthly report, posts, review follow-up, check-ins. We'll write these up as we do them.
